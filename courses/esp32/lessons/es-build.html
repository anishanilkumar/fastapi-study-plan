Everything in the course, in one device: a probe in the fridge that reports its temperature to the house, and a notice on your phone when the door's been left open. It ties together this course and the MQTT one.

The parts

  • An ESP32-C3 or S3 dev board, on a USB charger near the fridge.
  • A waterproof DS18B20 probe on a cable (the flat cable passes the door seal), and a 4.7 kΩ resistor.
  • Optional: a reed switch and magnet on the door, to one GPIO with the internal pull-up.

The topics

home/kitchen/fridge/status    online / offline       retained, will
home/kitchen/fridge/temp      {"c": 4.2, "ts": ...}  retained, QoS 0, on change ≥ 0.2 °C or every 5 min
home/kitchen/fridge/door      open / closed          retained, QoS 1, on change (debounced)
home/kitchen/fridge/boot      WDT_RESET …            retained

The firmware

Either way works; doing it both ways is the best practice there is.

  • MicroPython: the Wi-Fi and MQTT loop from lesson 8, the DS18B20 read from lesson 6, the door on an interrupt with debounce, a watchdog, NTP, and the reset cause published at boot.
  • ESPHome: the YAML from lesson 10 plus a binary_sensor for the door, with delayed_on_off as the debounce.

The house's side

The board publishes to the MQTT listener on jarvis's NATS leaf, as a fridge user allowed only home.kitchen.fridge.>. From there, it's NATS: a small Python program (on jarvis, or the VPS) subscribes to home.kitchen.fridge.>, keeps the door's state, and when the door has been open for more than two minutes, or the temperature is above 8 °C for ten, publishes a notice to notify.anish, which Friday's gateway already turns into a phone notification.

device ──MQTT──▶ jarvis (NATS leaf) ──NATS──▶ fridge-watch.py ──▶ notify.anish ──▶ Friday

Done means

  • Unplug the router for five minutes: the board reconnects on its own, and the status topic shows the gap.
  • Restart jarvis: the retained values are back, and the board reconnects.
  • Leave the door open: one notification, not one per bounce.
  • Pull the probe out: the device publishes an error rather than 85 °C or nothing.
  • Its YAML or .py files are in git, without secrets.
From the house

This is the shape every device will take: MQTT at the edge, into the leaf, and from there the same NATS subjects and Friday notices the rest of the house already uses. One device done end to end makes the next ten a matter of copying.

Trap

Putting the alert logic on the device. "Door open for two minutes" needs a timer that survives the device rebooting, and the threshold will change. Keep the device dumb (report facts), and put decisions in a program you can change in seconds.

Try it

Build it on your desk first, against a NATS server with MQTT on your Mac (the MQTT course's dry run), with a glass of ice water as the fridge.
